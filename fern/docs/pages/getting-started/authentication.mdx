6MM supports two account access models: integration with a partner system and a white-label platform. Use the entry provided by your platform. Account creation, authentication and profile management depend on the selected model.

<h2 id="registration-model">Choose your account model</h2>

| Area | Partner integration | White-label platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account source | The user has an account in the partner system, which binds or synchronizes the trading user. | The user belongs to the white-label platform's account system. |
| Access entry | The partner's app, website or embedded trading entry. | The white-label platform's branded domain and login page. |
| Authentication | The partner verifies the user and its backend obtains the required trading access credential. | Login, verification and account security follow the platform's enabled configuration. |
| Account management | The partner manages its user profile; the trading system maintains the required user mapping and trading records. | The white-label account system and authorized administrators manage user profiles, account status and security settings. |

<h2 id="login-method-1-partner-embedded-access">Partner integration</h2>

1. Sign in through the partner's account system.
2. The partner backend verifies the session and binds or identifies the corresponding trading user.
3. The backend obtains the required trading access credential or entry URL through the supported integration.
4. Open the embedded trading interface or authorized trading entry supplied by the partner.

Account access does not by itself make funds available for trading. Follow the partner's funding process and confirm the available perpetual-account balance before placing an order.

<h2 id="login-method-2-6mm-account-login-partner-issued-credentials">White-label platform access</h2>

Use the branded domain and account entry provided by the white-label platform. Whether self-registration is available, how accounts are opened, and which login or identity-verification methods are enabled depend on that platform's configuration.

The platform's account system and authorized administrators handle profile management, account status and security operations within their permissions. Follow the platform's verification process for password recovery, contact-information changes or account restrictions.

After signing in, prepare funds through the platform's supported funding process. For perpetual trading, transfer the required settlement asset from the funding account to the perpetual account and confirm the transfer result.

<h2 id="user-ownership-and-data-responsibility">Account and data responsibilities</h2>

The two models have different responsibility boundaries. Partner integrations retain partner-side account management while maintaining the user mapping and records required for trading. White-label platforms use their own account processes and administrative permissions to manage users.

The information collected, stored or displayed, identity-verification requirements and support responsibilities must be defined for the actual deployment. Do not apply the account-management assumptions of one model to every platform.

<h2 id="design-principles">Before entering trading</h2>

- Confirm that you are using the correct platform domain and account.
- Complete any identity or security checks required by that platform.
- Check that the account has trading access and review any restrictions.
- Confirm the available balance in the perpetual account; signing in does not transfer funds automatically.
- Contact your platform's support team if account access or identity verification needs attention.
