The fully managed wallet is for enterprises that want the platform to operate the signing infrastructure. The enterprise uses its workspace to handle daily funds, review activity and assign business responsibilities.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="divide-operational-responsibilities" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Divide operational responsibilities

| Party | Typical responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Operate the signing infrastructure and handle applicable platform risk review. |
| Enterprise | Set workspace permissions and approval rules, prepare payments, review balances and reconcile transactions with business records. |

The available tools depend on the workspace and permissions. The funds view groups wallet balances and the transaction views separate ledger entries, chain activity, deposits and withdrawals.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="work-with-your-payment-team" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Work with your payment team

An authorized operator prepares a withdrawal. Use the preview to check the funds, destination and applicable fees. A reviewer handles the task when the approval policy requires it. Some requests can also enter platform risk review. Follow the withdrawal record as it proceeds through signing and chain confirmation.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="example-of-a-merchant-payout" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Example of a merchant payout

A merchant reconciles customer deposits against its records and prepares a payout from its available balance. Its authorized reviewers handle the request if needed. Finance then compares the final withdrawal record and chain transaction with the merchant's payout reference.

For the day-to-day records used in this flow, see [balances and reconciliation](/mpc-wallet/balances-and-reconciliation).
