Receiving covers the incoming transfer and its deposit record. Collection moves assets from receiving addresses to a designated wallet under the applicable enterprise arrangement. Finance teams can track each step separately.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="prepare-receiving-details" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Prepare receiving details

Choose the network and copy the receiving address with any required memo or tag. When allocating an enterprise address for a business user, use the correct user identifier and record how the address is linked to your records. Check the network and asset with the sender before funds are transferred.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="follow-the-deposit" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Follow the deposit

Use the deposit record and chain transaction to check the incoming transfer. Chain visibility, confirmation and business credit can occur at different times. A deposit undergoing risk review may be held while its treatment is determined. Check the record before counting the amount as available.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="follow-enterprise-collection" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Follow enterprise collection

If collection is enabled, check the destination wallet and the applicable network policy. Depending on the configuration, the wallet can show collection tasks and their results. Match the collection transaction to the internal movement of funds and check the fees where relevant.

For example, if several customers pay into separate addresses, reconcile each deposit against the corresponding customer reference. Record the later movement to the designated wallet as an internal collection so your books reflect one incoming payment for each customer. See [balances and reconciliation](/mpc-wallet/balances-and-reconciliation).
