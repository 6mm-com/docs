This enterprise wallet is for businesses that want to operate some or all of their signing participants. It brings receiving addresses, wallet balances, fund movements and transfer reviews into an enterprise workspace alongside node management.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="choose-how-to-operate-signing-nodes" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Choose how to operate signing nodes

With self-custody, the enterprise holds the wallet's key fragments. With joint custody, the enterprise operates its own participants alongside platform participants. The available options and participant requirements are shown during setup. Confirm the ownership of backups, responsibility for recovery and expected device availability before creating wallets.

Where your role allows, the workspace provides views for MPC nodes and their trust information. Use the setup status to confirm when node enrollment and wallet initialization have reached the required checkpoints.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="organize-treasury-by-wallet-role" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Organize treasury by wallet role

An enterprise can distinguish receiving, outgoing payments, transit and reserve wallet roles. The funds team can review available balances and transaction records while authorized operators work with deposit addresses or initiate fund movements. Different wallet roles can have different signing configurations.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="example-of-separating-operating-funds-from-reserves" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Example of separating operating funds from reserves

A business receives deposits into dedicated receiving addresses and uses its collection arrangement to move funds to the designated wallet. The treasury team reviews the balance it needs for regular payments and keeps a separate reserve arrangement. When sending a larger payment or reallocating funds, the team checks the applicable approval rules and the availability of the required signers for the source wallet.

See [receiving and collection](/mpc-wallet/receiving-and-collection) and [payments and approvals](/mpc-wallet/payments-and-approvals).
