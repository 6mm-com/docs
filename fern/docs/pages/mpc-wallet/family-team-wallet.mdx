The family and team wallet is designed for people who manage a shared balance together. A workspace gives the group a common place to view assets and activity, organize member access and follow pending signing tasks.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="decide-who-does-what" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Decide who does what

Agree on who can view assets, start a transfer and manage membership. Administrators with the relevant permissions can invite and manage members in the current space. Invitations, workspace permissions and the devices that participate in a signature each serve a separate purpose.

For someone who will participate in signing, complete the applicable device enrollment and check the signing configuration. If member responsibilities change, review the device arrangement separately from the account permissions.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="follow-a-shared-transfer" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Follow a shared transfer

The initiating member prepares the destination, asset and amount. Designated signing participants review the request and act on their devices. The workspace's pending tasks show signing progress. The transaction record helps members verify the final result together.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="example-of-a-team-expense" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Example of a team expense

A small team holds working funds in one workspace. An authorized member prepares a supplier payment while another member can review the transaction details. The required devices participate in signing according to the wallet plan. After confirmation, the team uses the chain record to settle its internal expense record.

Before using the wallet for shared funds, agree on what happens if a participant is unavailable or leaves. The permitted options depend on the selected custody plan. See [security and recovery](/mpc-wallet/security-and-recovery).
