Use this sequence when a test environment is made available. Access links and installation packages should come from your onboarding contact; this guide does not provide a public signup or download endpoint.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="prepare-your-workspace" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Prepare your workspace

1. Confirm the product you will evaluate and the account or organization that owns its workspace.
2. Sign in using the supplied access route and complete the account security steps offered by that version.
3. Select the intended workspace. Check its product, member permissions and initialization status before operating assets.
4. For products with a custody choice, review the participant and recovery responsibilities before confirming it.

Do not confuse a login account, a workspace and an on-chain wallet. One account can have access to different workspaces, while each workspace determines its own product and operating context.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="complete-initialization" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Complete initialization

Depending on the product, setup can include enrolling devices or nodes, accepting participants, creating wallet keys and preparing backups. Follow the setup status for the selected workspace instead of treating one successful step as completion of the entire process.

The fully managed experience does not require the same customer node setup as an enterprise wallet with customer-held shares. Personal and team devices also have their own enrollment and signing flow.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="validate-a-small-test-flow" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Validate a small test flow

1. Confirm the enabled network and asset, including whether the environment is a test network.
2. Obtain a receiving address and any required memo or tag. Where address verification is offered, complete it before distributing the address.
3. Receive a small test amount and follow the deposit record until its result is known.
4. Prepare a small outgoing transfer. Review its destination, fees and required approvals or device signatures.
5. Compare the wallet record with the chain result and your own business records.

Before expanding the test, verify that the team can handle a rejected approval, unavailable signer, missing callback and uncertain transaction result. See [wallet operations](/mpc-wallet/wallet-operations) and [security and recovery](/mpc-wallet/security-and-recovery).
