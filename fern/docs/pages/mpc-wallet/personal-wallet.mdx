The personal wallet is for someone who manages their own digital assets and wants signing participation distributed across the devices or nodes in their wallet plan. In one workspace you can view assets, obtain receiving details, follow transfer progress and manage devices.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="set-up-the-wallet-and-devices" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Set up the wallet and devices

Select the personal workspace and review the available custody choices. Self-custody keeps the wallet's key fragments with the customer. Joint custody assigns a portion of the signing material to the platform. The setup screen shows the required participants for the option you choose.

Complete device enrollment and wallet initialization before funding the wallet. Review the recovery material and device replacement procedure for the selected plan. See [custody models](/mpc-wallet/custody-models) and [security and recovery](/mpc-wallet/security-and-recovery).

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="receive-and-send-assets" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Receive and send assets

1. Open the wallet's asset view and select the network for the incoming transfer. Copy the receiving address and any required memo or tag together.
2. Check the transaction history after sending funds. Match the network, asset and amount against the expected transfer.
3. For an outgoing transfer, review the destination, amount and fee in the transfer flow. Follow the signing task on the required devices and wait for the final transaction result.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="example-of-moving-funds-between-your-wallets" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Example of moving funds between your wallets

Suppose you want to move assets from another wallet into bitMPC. Obtain the receiving details for the intended network, verify them in both wallets and send a small test amount when you have evaluation access. Keep the transaction reference so you can locate the record later. When you send funds out, check that the signing participants for your plan are available.
