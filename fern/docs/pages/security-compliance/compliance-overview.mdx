6MM provides technology infrastructure and compliance-oriented support for authorized partners. Compliance requirements vary by jurisdiction and business model, so each launch should be reviewed against the partner's own obligations.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="what-this-section-covers" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## What this section covers

| Area | Description |
| --- | --- |
| License support | Guidance around supported license paths and required preparation materials. |
| Partner obligations | User onboarding, disclosures, local compliance controls, and customer relationship management. |
| Technical controls | Signing, logging, access control, webhook verification, and production monitoring. |
| Launch review | Checklist-based confirmation before exposing trading functionality to users. |

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="important-boundary" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Important boundary

6MM documentation helps partners prepare technical and operational controls. It does not replace legal advice, jurisdiction-specific compliance review, or the partner's own regulatory obligations.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="information-to-prepare" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Information to prepare

Before a compliance or launch review, document:

- Target countries or regions and the user types that will receive access.
- Product scope, including embedded trading, white-label delivery, liquidity, wallet, custody, or brokerage features.
- The entity responsible for user onboarding, identity checks, disclosures, custody, support, and incident communication.
- Data flows among partner frontend, partner backend, 6MM APIs, SDKs, webhooks, and operational systems.
- Credential storage, access control, audit logging, monitoring, and escalation owners.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="recommended-review-path" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Recommended review path

1. Use the [partner responsibility matrix](/security-compliance/partner-responsibilities) to assign ownership.
2. Review [license support](/security-compliance/license-support) against the target operating model.
3. Confirm the [security architecture](/security-compliance/security-architecture) and API/Webhook controls.
4. Complete the [production launch checklist](/resources/production-launch-checklist).

Legal and compliance conclusions must be approved by qualified advisors and the responsible partner entity. These pages describe integration preparation and responsibility boundaries, not a legal opinion or a guarantee that a particular license is available.
