## Your knowledge, your permissions

Each tenant manages its own content, team and conversations. Menu access depends on the account role. AI uses the supplied business knowledge; it does not automatically have access to exchange account balances, live orders or on-chain deposit status. Those capabilities require an explicit business data integration.

This tenant guide covers customer support. The separate [AI Hub](/ai-hub/overview) describes market and trading integration concepts; its MCP endpoints are not interchangeable with the customer support endpoint.
