## Understand support activity

Open **Statistics** and choose the relevant time range and available filters. Review real-time overview, conversations, tickets, satisfaction, messages, agents, feedback and knowledge statistics.

Use several metrics together. Conversation volume shows demand, ticket status shows follow-up work, and ratings provide feedback on individual experiences. A high knowledge view count alone does not prove an issue was resolved.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="compare-like-for-like-data" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Compare like-for-like data

Keep the same time range, timezone, filters and module scope when comparing results. Check the field labels rather than assuming all numbers count unique people. If a value differs from another screen, compare its definition and filtering first.

MCP can query statistics and help summarize trends. It cannot modify statistical records through the read-only statistics tools. Ask it to include the queried period and source metrics in the summary so the result can be checked in the console.


<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="choose-a-view-for-the-question" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Choose a view for the question

| Question | Useful views |
| --- | --- |
| What changed in support demand? | Messages, human conversations, bot activity and country distribution. |
| Is follow-up work accumulating? | Tickets and feedback, with the same reporting period. |
| How do visitors evaluate service? | Satisfaction, followed by a review of the underlying conversations. |
| Which help content needs attention? | FAQ and knowledge statistics, compared with repeated questions from real sessions. |

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="example-prepare-a-weekly-review" aria-hidden="true"></span>

### Example: prepare a weekly review

1. Select the agreed week and record the date range and active filters in the review.
2. Summarize demand, unfinished work and visitor feedback separately. Do not add unlike metrics together.
3. Open a small set of relevant conversations or tickets to understand the reasons behind the change.
4. Choose a concrete action: revise an unclear article, update a quick reply or review team handling. Compare the next period using the same definitions.

You can ask an MCP-connected AI to summarize the selected statistics and identify questions to investigate. Require it to name the period and distinguish observed figures from suggested explanations.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does a popular article mean it resolves most questions?">
Views and interactions describe activity, not guaranteed resolution. Read visitor questions and outcomes before making that conclusion.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why do two reports show different totals?">
Compare the time basis, filters and metric definition first. Session counts, messages and unique visitors are different quantities.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
