## Find an earlier conversation

Open **Conversation History**. Use the available user/session search, conversation type, rating, agent, category, language and time filters. Open the details to read the messages and session information.

The user-email list column prefers the contact email and otherwise uses the visitor email. The detail view retains both fields so agents can distinguish their sources. An empty value means that field was not collected; it does not prove a customer has no email account.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="review-service-quality" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Review service quality

Use the conversation, agent and rating together to understand the case. A reply shown as an AI message may come from a configured answer or matching stage rather than a large-model call. Compare the content with the current knowledge and use a flow test when investigating reply behavior.

Deletion is available only with the relevant permissions. Read the confirmation before deleting one or multiple records. MCP history queries use the authorized scope, and deletion requires confirmation; do not assume full tenant history is available to every agent.
