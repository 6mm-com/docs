Prediction fees must be read alongside the accepted order's odds and the fee schedule disclosed by your partner platform. This guide does not set a universal fee rate.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="distinguish-the-amounts" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Distinguish the amounts

| Item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Odds multiplier | Determines the gross winning return under the order terms |
| Target return parameter | A pricing-model input; not a separate fee transaction by itself |
| Separately disclosed service fee | A charge to reconcile if it applies to the product/order |
| Partner revenue settlement | A business-level allocation, not automatically another user order charge |

The operating model includes prediction platform service fees as a fee category. The existence of that category does not establish a rate, a charging moment, or whether a specific order is charged separately.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="check-before-submitting" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Check before submitting

- Whether a fee applies to the selected game and account.
- The fee basis and amount, and when it is charged.
- Whether the displayed return is before or after a separately charged fee.
- How fees are treated for refunded or voided orders.

If those terms are unclear, obtain the applicable schedule through [Partner Support](/resources/contact/support) before trading.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="reconcile-without-deducting-twice" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Reconcile without deducting twice

An odds parameter and a separate charge are different records. Do not deduct an inferred percentage from a payout that already reflects the accepted odds. Reconcile only the actual disclosed charges and transactions.

See [Payouts and PnL](/prediction/returns-pnl) and [Transfers and Transactions](/prediction/transfers-ledger).
