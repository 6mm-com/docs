## Do all games use rounds?

No. Up/Down uses scheduled rounds. Custom orders have independent durations and expiry times. Each Grid order refers to its own cell and valid window.

## Must the price finish inside my grid cell?

No. A qualifying touch or crossing during the cell's valid window is the condition. A touch after the window does not count. See [Prediction Grid](/prediction/grid).

## Does a moving grid change my accepted order?

No. Its accepted price interval, valid time window, and odds remain recorded even when the live grid recenters or refreshes.

## Is the displayed return all profit?

No. A winning total payout includes the stake component. At 1.85x, a 100 USDT winning stake returns 185 USDT before separate fees: 85 USDT is gross net profit. See [Payouts and PnL](/prediction/returns-pnl).

## Why is my available balance lower than my funding balance?

Pending prediction orders can reserve funds. Do not add that reservation again when computing total assets. See [Accounts and Balances](/prediction/accounts-balances).

## Why is a high-odds cell unavailable?

The grid rule makes a cell unavailable when its calculated odds exceed the configured maximum. Product switches, limits, timing, and account restrictions can also prevent an order.

## Does a 97% target return guarantee I receive 97% back?

No. It is a model parameter, not an individual payout or guaranteed long-term outcome. See [Odds Mechanisms](/prediction/odds).

## What if the result is delayed or my request times out?

Check the original order and transactions before submitting again. For missing price evidence or a dispute, retain the order's conditions and contact support. See [Refunds and Exceptions](/prediction/refunds-exceptions).

## Are prediction stakes included in perpetual trading turnover?

They are separate metrics. Prediction reporting distinguishes valid stake, payout, user PnL, refunds, and voids; it should not combine these amounts with perpetual or spot turnover.

## Can I use the perpetual API order examples for prediction?

Do not assume those endpoints accept prediction orders. Confirm the prediction interfaces and entry methods enabled for your integration through [Partner Support](/resources/contact/support).
