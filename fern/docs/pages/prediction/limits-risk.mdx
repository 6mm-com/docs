An offered game or quote does not guarantee that every proposed order can be accepted. Available funds, product configuration, and risk controls all affect acceptance.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="common-restrictions" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Common restrictions

| Restriction | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Game or symbol disabled | The selected product is unavailable for new orders |
| Period not enabled | That duration cannot be selected for the symbol |
| Single-order minimum/maximum | The stake must fall within the displayed permitted range |
| Cumulative stake or exposure control | Further orders can be restricted even when the single stake is valid |
| Odds deviation control | A quote may require review or prediction entry may be restricted |
| Grid quote above maximum | The cell is unavailable under the grid pricing rule |
| Closed betting window | A new order cannot be accepted into the closed window |

The management model includes alerts, review, prediction restrictions, and delayed order handling. The actual response depends on the triggered rule; do not assume every limit produces the same outcome.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="stake-and-model-risk" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Stake and model risk

A losing order with no payout loses its stake. Larger odds are not evidence of a better opportunity: they can reflect a lower estimated hit probability. A target return parameter does not guarantee an individual's return.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="configuration-changes" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## Configuration changes

Check the current product settings before a new order. For accepted Custom and Grid orders, retained odds and relevant order snapshots prevent a later screen refresh from redefining those accepted terms.

<span data-sixmm-canonical-anchor="when-an-order-is-unavailable" aria-hidden="true"></span>

## When an order is unavailable

Check the product switch, available balance, limits, quote, and timing. Do not repeatedly resubmit to bypass a restriction. Preserve the response and order identifier if one was created.
